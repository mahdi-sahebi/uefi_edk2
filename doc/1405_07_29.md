## Why this capture stops short of the menu

`FTWa-3` was emitted immediately before `delay_s(2)` in `FaultTolerantWritePei`. That function performed roughly four billion volatile arithmetic iterations for this one call. Earlier `delay_s(1)` calls also ran, so the final marker does **not** establish a flash-access fault; the capture may have ended while the artificial loop was still running. There was no useful serial checkpoint between that marker and the flash metadata reads.

The two `AcpiTimerLib.c(57)` assertions came from `DelayedDispatchDispatcher()` requesting the time even though its queue was empty. `BlSupportPei`, which creates `gUefiAcpiBoardInfoGuid`, had not yet run. The log continues after both assertions, so they were not the observed stopping point. Coreboot's earlier warnings and assertions also did not prevent the payload handoff. The flash-size mismatch and missing TPM remain separate platform issues to investigate if a later capture shows they affect DXE or BDS.

The current coreboot configuration selects ASRock SPC741D8, SMMSTORE v2, SOL UART `0x2f8`, the built-in UEFI Shell, and the EDK2 boot menu. The menu and Shell are therefore intended destinations after PEI/DXE complete.

## Changes made

- Removed the FTW artificial busy loops and their VGA-only progress code. FTW now logs HOB contents, working and spare ranges, workspace validation, recovery decisions, and PPI installation to SOL using `[EDK2-DIAG]` markers.
- FTW now checks missing or short HOBs, unsupported HOB versions, failed range lookups, invalid or overflowing ranges, and recovery HOB allocation before dereferencing or publishing data. Its spare scan has a defined lower bound.
- PEI skips time calculation when the delayed-dispatch queue is empty, so those early calls cannot trigger the missing ACPI board-info HOB assertion.
- Added or extended markers for `BlSupportPei` completion, DXE IPL entry and handoff, boot-menu readiness and selection, and Shell initialization. Existing console-connection markers remain in BDS.
